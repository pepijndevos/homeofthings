Tom worked on character analysis in classic literature with Maria. We focused on "To Kill a Mockingbird" and examined how Harper Lee develops Scout's character throughout the novel.

Initially, Tom found it challenging to identify subtle character changes and motivations. We spent time discussing how authors reveal character through actions, dialogue, and internal thoughts rather than direct description.

Through guided questions and discussion, Tom began to recognize Scout's growing awareness of social injustice and moral complexity. He was particularly interested in the relationship dynamics between Scout and Atticus.

By the end of the session, Tom was able to independently identify examples of character growth and explain their significance to the story's themes. He expressed interest in reading more novels with complex character development.
