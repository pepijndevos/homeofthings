Sarah and Alex worked together on a collaborative creative writing project with Maria. They co-authored a short story about time travel, with each student contributing alternating paragraphs.

The collaboration showcased both students' strengths - Sarah's descriptive abilities and Alex's talent for dialogue and character development. They learned to build on each other's ideas while maintaining narrative consistency.

We discussed the importance of communication in collaborative writing and techniques for maintaining a unified voice across multiple authors. Both students expressed interest in continuing collaborative projects in the future.
