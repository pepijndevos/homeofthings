Alex dove deep into electromagnetic theory with Bob. We covered electric and magnetic fields, their relationships, and how they propagate as electromagnetic waves.

Starting with simple electric field calculations, Alex quickly moved to understanding magnetic field interactions. The concept of electromagnetic induction was particularly fascinating to him.

His mathematical skills served him well when working through Maxwell's equations. Alex expressed strong interest in continuing with advanced electromagnetic theory.
