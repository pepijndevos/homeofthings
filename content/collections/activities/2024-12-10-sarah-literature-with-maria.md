Sarah spent time analyzing Robert Frost's poetry with Maria. We focused on understanding metaphors, symbolism, and the deeper meanings behind seemingly simple verses.

Starting with "The Road Not Taken," Sarah demonstrated excellent critical thinking skills when discussing the poem's true meaning versus popular interpretation. She identified the ironic tone and understood the complexity of the speaker's reflection.

We also examined "Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening" and discussed themes of duty, beauty, and mortality. Sarah made connections between the poems and showed understanding of Frost's use of nature as metaphor.

Her written analysis was thoughtful and well-structured. Sarah expressed interest in exploring more contemporary poetry in future sessions.
